About Me

From operational data to cloud platforms

I’m a data analyst based in Philadelphia who increasingly works across data engineering, backend development, and Azure infrastructure. I build the systems behind the analysis: ingestion paths, governed models, APIs, automations, deployment infrastructure, and monitoring.

At Neta Scientific, I rebuilt the Catalog Team DataHub in Microsoft Fabric and serve as the infrastructure and backend engineer for NetaCAP, a private internal asset-management platform. Those projects let me connect business context with the engineering practices needed to run dependable systems.
