About Me

From spreadsheets to systems people rely on

I’m a data analyst based in Philadelphia working at the intersection of catalog operations, pricing data, and internal data systems. I graduated from Drexel University in June 2025 with a B.S. in Information Systems, started out as an analyst in the energy industry, spent time in IT support, and then joined Neta Scientific on the Catalog Team.

My day-to-day still includes supplier data, pricing analysis, customer updates, and catalog operations — but the role has grown well past traditional analysis. A large part of my job now is spotting inefficient workflows and building the internal systems that replace them. The biggest of those is the Catalog Team Data Hub: an internal platform I designed and continue to build to move our team off scattered spreadsheets and onto centralized, traceable, database-backed processes.

Building it has pulled me into platform engineering and DevOps. I design PostgreSQL schemas, build FastAPI services, develop ETL pipelines, ship browser-based tools, monitor application health, manage Docker deployments, and work alongside IT on Microsoft Entra SSO, Graph integration, VPN connectivity, and cloud hosting. What I enjoy most is owning the whole arc — finding the problem, designing the solution, building it, deploying it, and improving it once people actually depend on it.

Outside of work I’m 27 bottles deep into a fragrance collection that shows no sign of stopping, play basketball, and game with friends — mostly Satisfactory, Rocket League, Marvel Rivals, and Brawlhalla. I keep up with sneaker and streetwear culture, and I’m eyeing a watch collection while actively trying not to fall down another expensive rabbit hole.
