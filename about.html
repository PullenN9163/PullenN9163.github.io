About Me

Turning data work into platform ownership

I’m a data analyst based in Philadelphia building toward data and cloud platform engineering. I graduated from Drexel University in June 2025 with a B.S. in Information Systems, started in energy operations analytics and IT support, and now work across catalog operations, data platforms, backend systems, automation, and Azure infrastructure at Neta Scientific.

I rebuilt the Catalog Team DataHub as a live Microsoft Fabric platform with Lakehouse storage, pipelines, notebooks, dataflows, semantic models, Power BI reporting, and a published Fabric app. The work connects the context I have as an analyst with the engineering needed to make data repeatable, governed, and useful to a team.

I also serve as the infrastructure and backend engineer for NetaCAP, a private internal asset-management platform. I design and configure managed Azure services, backend boundaries, asynchronous processing, persistence, identity-based access, deployment infrastructure, and observability. What I enjoy most is owning the whole arc — understanding the workflow, designing the system, deploying it, and improving it once people rely on it.

Outside of work I’m 27 bottles deep into a fragrance collection that shows no sign of stopping, play basketball, and game with friends — mostly Satisfactory, Rocket League, Marvel Rivals, and Brawlhalla. I keep up with sneaker and streetwear culture, and I’m eyeing a watch collection while actively trying not to fall down another expensive rabbit hole.
