Business Intelligence

A Power BI dashboard built on 2024 eBay men’s fragrance sales data — a hobby turned into a market analysis.

Fun fact about me: I am obsessed with colognes and smell goods — oils, lotions, all of it. That collection is what motivated me to research the perfume market and find a way to demonstrate my skills through something I actually care about.

I built a Power BI dashboard to visualize the analysis I did on 2024 eBay sales data for men’s fragrances, looking at which brands moved the most units, how sales split across concentration types, and where in the country products were selling.
