Analytics & Visualization

A PostgreSQL-backed analysis workflow and Looker Studio dashboard for spotting player prop bets, with deeper statistical work in R.

The idea for this project came from my favorite sport. I love watching and betting on the NBA, and I wanted to demonstrate analysis and visualization skills on a topic I’m actually passionate about.

The goal was to visualize player data to identify potential bets based on specific statistics — for example, taking the over on 20 points for Shai given a 30 points-per-game average. I gathered a full player dataset and loaded it into my PostgreSQL database, then connected that database to Google Looker Studio for the interactive dashboard. I used R for the more advanced analysis, like comparing points-per-game distribution in the regular season against the playoffs.

Regular season vs. playoff PPG distribution

R
library(ggplot2)
regular_season <- read.csv("/Volumes/MySQL_Server/NBA Data/regular_season_cleaned.csv")
playoff        <- read.csv("/Volumes/MySQL_Server/NBA Data/playoff_cleaned.csv")

# Compares Regular Season vs Playoffs
ggplot() +
  geom_histogram(aes(x = regular_season$PTS), fill="blue", alpha=0.5, bins=20) +
  geom_histogram(aes(x = playoff$PTS), fill="red", alpha=0.5, bins=20) +
  labs(title="Regular Season vs Playoff PPG Distribution",
       x="Points Per Game", y="Frequency")

Top 10 players by total contribution (P+R+A)

R
nba %>%
  arrange(desc(`P+R+A`)) %>%        # Sort by total contribution
  slice_head(n = 10) %>%            # Take top 10 players
  ggplot(aes(x = reorder(NAME, `P+R+A`), y = `P+R+A`, fill = TEAM)) +
  geom_col(show.legend = FALSE) +
  coord_flip() +
  labs(
    title = "Top 10 Players by Total Contribution (P+R+A)",
    subtitle = "2024-25 NBA Regular Season",
    x = "Player",
    y = "Points + Rebounds + Assists"
  ) +
  theme_minimal() +
  theme(
    plot.title = element_text(face = "bold", size = 14),
    plot.subtitle = element_text(size = 11),
    axis.text.y = element_text(face = "bold")
  )
